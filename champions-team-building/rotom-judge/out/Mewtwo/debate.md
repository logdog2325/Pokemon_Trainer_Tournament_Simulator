# Rotom Judge: Mega Mewtwo X vs Mega Mewtwo Y

> **Winner: Mega Mewtwo Y** — Mega Mewtwo X **82** / Mega Mewtwo Y **87** (out of 100)

| | Mega Mewtwo X | Mega Mewtwo Y |
|---|---|---|
| Total | 82 | 87 |
| Evidence / Relevance / Rebuttal / Honesty | 35/40, 21/25, 16/20, 10/15 | 36/40, 22/25, 17/20, 12/15 |

Rubric: Evidence 40 (minus 5 per checked claim that fails), Relevance to the Reg M-C board 25, Rebuttal 20, Honesty 15.

## Round 1 — Opening arguments
### For Mega Mewtwo X
Thesis: in the Reg M-C format as it actually exists, Mega Mewtwo X is the better Mega form. It survives the priority attacks and physical Dark and Bug moves that make up most of the usage-weighted board. Its OHKOs come from moves that always hit. And its Fighting typing hits Incineroar and Kingambit, the two most-used Pokemon that stop a Psychic attacker. Mega Y wins more on paper and fewer on the field.

**1. Y's OHKO lead depends on inaccurate moves; X's does not (fact sheet).** On paper Y OHKOs 13/21 and X OHKOs 9/21. But 8 of Y's 13 need Focus Blast (70%), Fire Blast (85%) or Thunder (70%): Kingambit, M-Golisopod, Gholdengo, Pelipper, Whimsicott, Archaludon, M-Froslass and M-Tyranitar. Only 5 of Y's OHKOs use a 100%-accurate move. X gets 8 OHKOs with 100% moves: Sneasler, M-Salamence (15/16), Garchomp, Pelipper, Whimsicott, Arcanine-Hisui, M-Tyranitar and M-Staraptor (14/16). Its ninth, M-Froslass, uses Stone Edge (80%). In a best-of-3 set, the OHKOs that always land are the ones that count.

**2. Y is OHKO'd by the most-used physical attackers; X is not (engine and fact sheet).** Both forms below use the same offensive spread: 2 HP, 32 in the attacking stat, 32 Speed.
- Kingambit (22.44%) Sucker Punch: into Y 99.5-118% (14/16). Into X 36.6-44.3%.
- Kingambit Kowtow Cleave: into Y 119.1-142.1%, a guaranteed OHKO. Into X 44.8-53%.
- Mega Golisopod (18.49%) First Impression: into Y 179.2-210.4%. Into X 66.7-79.8%.
- Rillaboom (37.18%) Wood Hammer in Grassy Terrain: into Y 102.7-122.4%. Into X 77.6-92.3%.
- Mega Tyranitar Crunch: into Y 129-154.1%. Into X 49.2-57.9%.

Overall Y is OHKO'd by 7/21 board threats and X by 4/21 (fact sheet). Y's losses sit on the highest-usage Pokemon, and Sucker Punch and Kowtow Cleave hit Y harder than any of X's four checks hit X.

**3. X's Fighting STAB beats the Dark types that wall Psychic (engine).** Y's Psystrike does nothing to Incineroar or Kingambit, and its answers to them are 70% Focus Blasts. X answers them with 100%-accurate moves:
- Drain Punch into Incineroar (26.77%): 87.1-104%. With Helping Hand, 130.7-155.9%, a guaranteed KO.
- Drain Punch into Kingambit, with its Chople Berry counted: 70.5-84.1%. With Helping Hand, 105.8-126.1%, a guaranteed KO.

Drain Punch also heals X while it attacks. It gets the same neutral 76-91% on Archaludon and Mega Baxcalibur, and a Helping Hand KO on both.

**4. X takes Fake Out and Knock Off better, and Steadfast helps (engine).** Incineroar's Knock Off does 25.1-30.1% to X and 65.6-78.7% to Y. Every Fake Out from Incineroar, Sneasler or Rillaboom that flinches X triggers Steadfast, which raises X's 200 Speed to 300 for the rest of the game. At that point X outspeeds the whole board without needing Tailwind.

**5. X's Speed is already enough (fact sheet).** At 200 Speed (Jolly, 32 Speed), X outspeeds all 21 board threats, the same as Y does at 211. Y's extra Speed only matters against Mega Mewtwo X itself and Mega Greninja. Neither is on the board.

**Team role and partners.** X works as a physical attacker next to a Helping Hand and Follow Me partner, or next to Psychic Terrain. Indeedee-F provides both, and its Psychic Terrain boosts Zen Headbutt to 62.3-73.4% on Rillaboom and 128.1-151.9% on Mega Staraptor (engine). Whimsicott is another option: Tailwind plus Helping Hand, with Prankster, which makes Drain Punch KO Incineroar and Kingambit. The 4 threats that OHKO X are Mega Salamence, Mega Staraptor, Sylveon and Gholdengo. Each has an answer on X's own moveset or on a teammate:
- Mega Salamence: X outspeeds it (200 vs 158) and Ice Punch KOs 15/16.
- Mega Staraptor: X outspeeds it (200 vs 178), and Zen Headbutt in Psychic Terrain KOs.
- Sylveon and Gholdengo: a partner with Wide Guard, or a Steel type, covers them.

**Pre-empting Y's best point: raw special damage and 13 OHKOs.** The number looks strong until you count accuracy and bulk. Five of Y's OHKOs are on Focus Blast (Kingambit, Incineroar is not even one, Archaludon, M-Tyranitar) or Thunder, where a single miss loses the turn. Two of them, Pelipper and Whimsicott, carry Focus Sash, so neither form actually OHKOs them. And Y is OHKO'd in return by Kingambit's Sucker Punch, which goes first no matter how fast Y is. Y's 211 Speed cannot outrun priority.

One weakness I will admit: X is physical, so Incineroar's Intimidate matters. At -1 Attack, Helping Hand Drain Punch still does 86.1-104% to Incineroar (engine). Y has no equivalent answer to Kingambit at any stat stage. X is the form that gets through the Pokemon people actually use.

### For Mega Mewtwo Y
**Thesis:** Mega Mewtwo Y is the better Mega. It is the fastest Mewtwo, it OHKOs more of the Reg M-C board, it is far harder to hit on the special side, and its partners fix its weak points in ways Mega X cannot copy.

**1. It OHKOs more of the board: 13/21 threats vs Mega X's 9/21 (fact sheet, no item).**
Timid 2/32 SpA/32 Spe gives 246 SpA. Here is what it deletes: Sneasler (Psystrike 443-522%), M-Salamence (Ice Beam 163-194%), Garchomp (Ice Beam 171-203%) and Arcanine-Hisui (Earth Power 195-230%). It also takes out Gholdengo (Fire Blast 109-129%), M-Golisopod (Fire Blast 160-191%), Kingambit (Focus Blast 102-121%) and Archaludon (Focus Blast 144-170%). Mega X cannot OHKO Kingambit (Brick Break 70.5-84.1%), Gholdengo (Fire Punch 71-84%) or Golisopod (76.9-92.3%). Those three together make up 56% usage. The accuracy cost is real: Fire Blast is 85%, Focus Blast 70%.

**2. It is the fastest Mewtwo: 211 vs 200 Speed (fact sheet).**
Both forms outspeed all 21 board threats. Y also beats Mega X itself, plus Mega Manectric and Mega Lopunny (205) and Mega Delphox (204), all of which outspeed X.

**3. Intimidate does not touch it.**
Incineroar is the #3 threat at 26.77% usage. I ran Mega X at -1 Atk in the engine. At -1 X loses its kills:
- Incineroar: Brick Break 57.4-69.3%
- M-Salamence: Ice Punch 66.7-79.6%
- Garchomp: Ice Punch drops to 86.5-103.8% (3/16 rolls KO)

Mega Y's special numbers stay the same. With Helping Hand, Focus Blast OHKOs Incineroar 120.3-142.6% (engine).

**4. Much better special bulk and neutral Flying/Fairy (fact sheet).**
Mega X is weak to Fairy and Flying. The same hits on each form:

| Hit | on Mega X | on Mega Y |
|---|---|---|
| Sylveon Hyper Beam | OHKO, 184-217% | 78.7-92.9% |
| M-Staraptor Brave Bird | OHKO, 105-125% | 69.4-82.5% |
| Whimsicott Moonblast | 63.4-75.4% | 27.9-32.8% |
| Indeedee-F Expanding Force | 35-41% | 14.8-17.5% |
| Pelipper's best hit | Hurricane 83-99.5% | Weather Ball 49.7-59% |

**5. Its amplifiers are real and on-format (engine).**
Mewtwo cannot hold Life Orb, and neither form can, because the stone uses the item slot. So the boosts come from the field and from partners:
- Psychic Terrain + Helping Hand Psystrike: Milotic 122.8-145.5% KO, Rillaboom 116.9-138.6% KO
- Helping Hand Psystrike on Sylveon: 110.2-130.5% KO
- Helping Hand Dark Pulse on Indeedee-F: 100-118.6% KO
- Fire Blast in sun: Rillaboom 110.1-130.4% KO, M-Metagross 138-162.6% KO

For comparison, Rillaboom is the #1 threat at 37.18% usage, and no-item Mega X only does 63.8-75.4% to it.

**Team role and partners**
Mega Y is a fast special sweeper that fires first in every slot.
- **Indeedee-F (Psychic Surge, Follow Me, Helping Hand)** is the main partner. It turns Psystrike into the Rillaboom and Milotic KOs listed above. Its Psychic Terrain also blocks priority against grounded Mewtwo Y. That shuts off the priority hits that exploit Y's lower physical bulk: M-Golisopod's First Impression (+2, 179-210% on Y) and Kingambit's Sucker Punch. Follow Me soaks the Dark and Bug attacks aimed at it.
- **Torkoal (Drought, 20 Speed)** is the alternative partner. It needs no Mega slot and gives the sun Fire Blast numbers above.

**Pre-empting Mega X's best point: "Y is OHKO'd by 7/21, X only by 4/21."**
Look at who those 7 are:
- **Slow threats that Y hits first and OHKOs:** Kingambit (70 Speed; Focus Blast KO, and guaranteed with Helping Hand), M-Golisopod (60; Fire Blast 160%), Arcanine-Hisui (156; Earth Power 195%) and M-Tyranitar (91; Focus Blast 153%).
- **Rillaboom:** it only KOs Y in its own Grassy Terrain (102.7-122.4%). Y still moves first, at 211 vs 105.
- **The remaining two:** M-Salamence and M-Baxcalibur. Y outspeeds both. Salamence takes Ice Beam 163%, and Baxcalibur takes Focus Blast 89-106%.

In short, most of Y's "defensive losses" are to threats it moves before and OHKOs. Mega X's losses are to Sylveon, Staraptor and Salamence, which hit it for super-effective damage through types it cannot change. It is also weakened by the Intimidate it faces from the #3 threat on the board. Mega Y has more kills, more Speed and less exposure: it is the better Mega.

## Round 2 — Rebuttals
### Mega Mewtwo X responds
**Rebuttal (Mega Mewtwo X)**

**1. "13/21 OHKOs" is really 11, and 6 of those depend on accuracy.** The engine's board gives Pelipper and Whimsicott Focus Sash, so Y's Thunder (329.9-391.2%) and Fire Blast (156.2-185.4%) leave both at 1 HP. That applies to X too, which drops to 7. Look at the OHKOs that always land. Y keeps 5: Sneasler, M-Salamence, Garchomp, Arcanine-H and one more. X keeps 6: Sneasler, M-Salamence (15/16), Garchomp, Arcanine-H, M-Tyranitar and M-Staraptor (14/16). Y's other 6 need Focus Blast (70%) or Fire Blast (85%). I concede one point: the engine does count Chople Berry, so Y's Focus Blast OHKOs Kingambit at 102.4-120.8% even through the berry. That OHKO is real, but it still lands only 70% of the time.

**2. Psychic Terrain only blocks priority; Kingambit still OHKOs Y.** They are right that Indeedee-F's terrain stops Sucker Punch and First Impression on a grounded Y. But Kowtow Cleave in Psychic Terrain still does 119.1-142.1% to Y (engine). Y moves first and hits with Focus Blast 70% of the time. The other 30%, Y dies. Mega X takes 44.8-53% from the same hit.

Their plan also runs into Rillaboom. Indeedee-F and Rillaboom both have 105 Speed on the board, and the slower setter's terrain is the one that stays. Both set terrain on entry, so that becomes a 50/50 speed tie. When Grassy Terrain wins, Wood Hammer OHKOs Y (102.7-122.4%). When Psychic Terrain wins, it does 79.2-94%. Y's main partner only protects it half the time against the #1 threat.

**3. Intimidate: I concede this, but look at the whole picture.** Their -1 numbers are correct: Drain Punch does 57.4-69.3% to Incineroar. I also have to correct my own opening. Helping Hand Drain Punch at -1 does 86.1-104%, and only 3/16 rolls KO. Y's Helping Hand Focus Blast does 120.3-142.6% if it hits, so into an Intimidate Incineroar, Y is the better form. However, without Helping Hand, Y's Focus Blast does only 80.2-95% to Incineroar, so Y also needs the partner. And Incineroar's Knock Off does 65.6-78.7% to Y against 25.1-30.1% to X. Y wins that trade only 70% of the time.

**4. Special bulk: I concede the numbers.** Their table is accurate. Sylveon's Hyper Beam and Mega Staraptor's Brave Bird OHKO X. But X outspeeds Staraptor (200 vs 178), and Zen Headbutt in Psychic Terrain OHKOs it. Sylveon's Hyper Beam is a spread move, which Wide Guard stops. Y's worst matchups are Kingambit, Mega Golisopod and Rillaboom, which have 22-37% usage each. X's are fewer and on lower-usage threats.

**5. Speed.** Mega Manectric, Mega Lopunny and Mega Delphox are not on the board. Against all 21 threats, 211 Speed and 200 Speed outspeed exactly the same set.

**Summary.** Y does more damage when Focus Blast hits and Indeedee-F wins the terrain tie. X does its job without either: it OHKOs more with 100%-accurate moves and takes only 44.8-53% from Kowtow Cleave, against Y's 119.1-142.1%.

Mega Mewtwo X still wins because more of its KOs land every time and it survives the Pokemon people actually use, so it loses fewer games to a missed Focus Blast or a lost terrain tie.

### Mega Mewtwo Y responds
**Rebuttal: Mega Mewtwo Y**

**1. X's priority numbers ignore the partner it named.** Kingambit's Sucker Punch (99.5-118%, 14/16) and Golisopod's +2 First Impression (179-210%) are correct engine numbers in a vacuum. But Y's listed partner is Indeedee-F, and X's opening names the same partner. Psychic Terrain blocks every priority move aimed at a grounded target (confirmed-facts.md). Mega Y is grounded, so both hits fail. That also covers Rillaboom's Grassy Glide and every Fake Out. The Fake Out point cuts against X: X also wants Indeedee, and under Indeedee's terrain Fake Out never flinches X, so Steadfast never triggers. I concede the non-priority hit. Kowtow Cleave OHKOs Y at 119-142%. Y still moves first (211 vs 70), and a Focus Blast miss loses Y.

**2. The Dark-type answer falls apart at -1.** X's own concession matters more than it admits. Incineroar and Kingambit are often brought together, and X's plan against Kingambit needs Helping Hand. I ran it in the engine with Kingambit's Chople Berry counted:
- X at -1, Helping Hand Drain Punch into Kingambit: **71-84.1%, no KO**
- X at -1, unboosted: 47.3-56%
- X at -1, Helping Hand into Incineroar: only 3/16 rolls KO (86.1-104%)

Y's answers ignore Intimidate. Helping Hand Focus Blast does 153.6-181.2% to Kingambit through Chople and 120.3-142.6% to Incineroar. Unboosted Focus Blast OHKOs Kingambit at 102.4-120.8%. So Y's answer is a 70% roll to KO. After one Intimidate, X's answer is close to a 0% roll to KO Kingambit.

**3. The accuracy argument: partly conceded, partly overstated.** X is right that only 5 of Y's 13 OHKOs use 100%-accurate moves, and that the Focus Sash on Pelipper and Whimsicott removes those KOs for both forms. I accept both. But three of X's own "sure" KOs are also rolls:
- M-Salamence: 15/16
- M-Staraptor: 14/16
- M-Froslass: Stone Edge at 80%

X's clean lead also falls apart in the matchups that matter by usage:
- **Rillaboom (#1, 37.18%):** X's best hit is 63.8-75.4%. Y's Psystrike in terrain alone does 77.8-92.3%, and Psychic Terrain plus Helping Hand OHKOs.
- **Gholdengo and M-Golisopod:** X needs two hits on each. Y has an 85% OHKO on each.

A 70-85% chance of a KO still beats a 0% chance.

**4. Bulk trade.** I concede Knock Off: it does 65.6-78.7% to Y against 25-30% to X. Y's 7/21 physical exposure is real, and it is the price of the form. But the threats that OHKO X are Sylveon (184-217%), Staraptor and Gholdengo. X can only handle them by adding Wide Guard or a Steel partner, which takes up a slot. Y's exposure is mostly to slow Pokemon it moves before, and its partner's terrain blocks the priority.

**Conclusion:** Mega Y keeps its damage through Intimidate, moves first, and under its own Psychic Terrain it cannot be hit by the priority moves X's case depends on. It is therefore the Mega that converts against the real Reg M-C board, while X loses its main answer to Kingambit after one Intimidate.

## ⚖️ The Judge's Opinion
_Having heard both openings and both rebuttals, Rotom Judge rules on which Mega is better, and why._

Every number both sides cited reproduces exactly. The case is decided by which weaknesses a partner can fix.

Y's physical frailty is real. Kowtow Cleave OHKOs it through Psychic Terrain (119.1-142.1%), and Knock Off does 65.6-78.7%. But
its priority deaths (Sucker Punch 14/16, First Impression 179-210%, Grassy Glide) are removed by Psychic Terrain on a grounded Y.
A's strongest counter was the 105-vs-105 Indeedee/Rillaboom terrain tie. That is fixable, because a Relaxed or Quiet
0-Speed Indeedee-F runs at 94 (engine), so it is slower than Rillaboom and wins the lead terrain every time. Y also keeps its
damage through Intimidate, and its PT+HH Psystrike answers the #1 threat, Rillaboom (116.9-138.6%), which X can only
dent (63.8-75.4%).

X's central claim, that Fighting STAB beats the Dark types, fails against the most common Dark type itself. Incineroar's
Intimidate drops X's Helping Hand Drain Punch on Kingambit to 71-84.1%, and Incineroar and Kingambit are the two most-used
Dark types. A also made a mechanics error: Sylveon's Hyper Beam is single-target, so Wide Guard does not cover X's worst
matchup. A's Fake Out/Steadfast point also conflicts with its own Psychic Terrain partner, which blocks Fake Out on X.
A was right about accuracy: 8 of Y's 13 OHKOs need a 70-85% move. That is why the margin is narrow. B used it well by
choosing Helping Hand plus Psychic Terrain boosts that turn 100%-accurate Psystrike into KOs. Also, Aura Sphere
(never misses) with Helping Hand KOs Kingambit at 102.9-121.7% (engine), so Y's Kingambit answer does not have to be a
70% Focus Blast.

### 🏆 Conclusion
**Rotom Judge rules that Mega Mewtwo Y is the better Mega** (Mega Mewtwo X 82, Mega Mewtwo Y 87).

Mega Mewtwo Y wins narrowly (87-82) in this hypothetical matchup. Every damage number either side cited checks out in the engine. X really is much bulkier against physical hits (Kowtow Cleave 44.8-53% vs Y's 119.1-142.1%), and 8 of Y's 13 OHKOs rely on 70-85% moves. But X's main claim, that Fighting STAB answers the Dark types, breaks against Incineroar's Intimidate: Helping Hand Drain Punch on Kingambit drops to 71-84.1%, no KO. A also made a mechanics error: Sylveon's Hyper Beam is single-target, so Wide Guard does not stop it. Y's priority weakness is fixable. Psychic Terrain blocks priority on the grounded Y, and a 0-Speed Relaxed Indeedee-F (94 Speed) is slower than Rillaboom (105), so it wins the terrain on the lead. Terrain plus Helping Hand Psystrike also KOs the #1 threat Rillaboom (116.9-138.6%), and Helping Hand Aura Sphere KOs Kingambit (102.9-121.7%) with no accuracy roll. X is the physically bulky, Intimidate-sensitive Helping Hand attacker. Y is a Psychic Terrain hyper-offense sweeper that must avoid Kowtow Cleave and Knock Off.

## The teams

It stays in against Dark and Bug priority and threatens with 100%-accurate coverage (Zen Headbutt, Drain Punch, Ice Punch, Earthquake). Pair it with Helping Hand to reach the Incineroar and Kingambit KOs, and with Psychic Terrain for Zen Headbutt. The team must handle Intimidate and the Fairy/Flying/Ghost hits that OHKO X. Do not count on Steadfast when running Psychic Terrain, because the terrain blocks Fake Out on X.
