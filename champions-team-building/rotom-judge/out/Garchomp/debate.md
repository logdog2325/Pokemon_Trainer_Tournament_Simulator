# Rotom Judge: Mega Garchomp vs Mega Garchomp Z

> **Winner: Mega Garchomp** — Mega Garchomp **75** / Mega Garchomp Z **68** (out of 100)

| | Mega Garchomp | Mega Garchomp Z |
|---|---|---|
| Total | 75 | 68 |
| Evidence / Relevance / Rebuttal / Honesty | 30/40, 17/25, 16/20, 12/15 | 25/40, 19/25, 14/20, 10/15 |

Rubric: Evidence 40 (minus 5 per checked claim that fails), Relevance to the Reg M-C board 25, Rebuttal 20, Honesty 15.

---
## Round 1 — Opening arguments
### For Mega Garchomp
**Opening argument: Mega Garchomp (Garchompite, Sand Force, Dragon/Ground)**

**Thesis:** Mega Garchomp keeps Ground STAB and 170 base Attack, and adds Sand Force. That makes it the stronger spread-damage Mega of the two against the Reg M-C board. It is also the bulkier one physically. Mega Garchomp Z trades that power away for Speed it mostly doesn't need.

**1. Ground STAB is the whole point of the Garchomp line, and Mega Z loses it.**
Mega Z is pure Dragon, so its Earthquake has no STAB. From my engine run (Jolly 2/32/32, spread already x0.75):
- Against Incineroar (26.77% usage), Mega's Earthquake does 80.2-95% and Mega Z's does 44.6-52.5%.
- Against Gholdengo, Mega does 95.9-113.6% (11/16 rolls KO). Mega Z does 53.3-62.7%.
- Against Kingambit, Mega does 63.8-75.4% and Mega Z does 34.8-41.5%.

Mega Garchomp hits both opposing slots for nearly double the damage every turn.

**2. In sand, Mega's Earthquake OHKOs part of the board while hitting both targets (fact sheet plus engine).**
Sand Force is x1.3. The fact sheet shows Mega's OHKOs going from 6/21 to 9/21 in sand, with no item. Spread Earthquake in sand OHKOs:
- Sneasler (34.29%): 195.5-229.9%
- Incineroar: 104.5-123.8%
- Gholdengo: 124.9-147.9%
- Arcanine-Hisui: 281.4-335.5%

It also has KO rolls on Archaludon (7/16) and M-Froslass (8/16). Mega Z's best no-item physical result on Sneasler is Scale Shot at 84.1-101.9% (1/16), and that move is only 90% accurate.

**3. With Helping Hand, one sand Earthquake KOs half the board (engine).**
Spread Earthquake in sand with Helping Hand OHKOs 9 of 21 board threats:
- Sneasler, Incineroar, Gholdengo, Arcanine-Hisui
- Kingambit: 124.2-146.9%
- Archaludon: 135.9-161.3%
- M-Froslass: 136.7-163.3%
- M-Metagross: 125.7-150.3%
- M-Tyranitar: 101.9-120.8%

It also KOs Sylveon on 13/16 rolls. That is a turn-one double knockout against the top 5 threats by usage, while Mega Z is limited to single-target attacks.

**4. Physical bulk against the metagame's physical attackers (fact sheet).**
Both forms have 185 HP, but Mega has 135 Def to Mega Z's 105.
- Sneasler Close Combat: 50.3-58.9% on Mega, 63.8-75.1% on Mega Z.
- Arcanine-Hisui Head Smash: 36.8-43.8% on Mega. On Mega Z it is 94.6-111.9%, KO on 11/16 rolls.
- Kingambit Kowtow Cleave: 39.5-47% on Mega, 50.8-60.5% on Mega Z.
- M-Salamence Dragon Claw: 78.9-94.1% on Mega. It OHKOs Mega Z (100.5-120%).
- Opposing Garchomp's Dragon Claw KOs Mega on only 3/16 rolls. It KOs Mega Z outright (110.8-131.9%).

Mega Garchomp survives the physical Dragon attacks that OHKO Mega Z.

**5. Mega's Speed is already enough (fact sheet).**
Jolly 32 gives 158. That outspeeds 15 of the 21 board threats, including Gholdengo (144), Arcanine-Hisui (156), Archaludon (123) and M-Metagross (148). Of the six that are faster, Mega already OHKOs Sneasler, Whimsicott and M-Froslass. Only M-Salamence ties.

**Team role and partners**
- **Main partner: Hippowdon (Sand Stream, base form).** It sets sand again every time it switches in, and it doesn't use up the one Mega slot. It also takes our Earthquake well: a Sand Force spread Earthquake does only 30.7-36.3% to Impish 32/32 Hippowdon (engine).
- **Alternative: Tyranitar, paired with Protect.** Its 61 base Speed makes it the slower setter against a Pelipper lead. Because the last weather write wins, sand stays up.
- **Other slots:** Flying or Levitate teammates such as Corviknight or Rotom-Wash, plus a Helping Hand user, round out a sand spread-damage core.

**Pre-empting Mega Z's best argument: 223 Speed and no 4x Ice weakness**
- Mega Z outspeeds all 21 board threats. But Mega already outspeeds 15, and it OHKOs three of the six threats that are faster.
- Mega Z's extra Speed buys only single-target hits. Its best no-item OHKO count is 7/21 with Timid Special, and that relies on Draco Meteor (90% accurate) and Fire Blast (85% accurate). Mega reaches 9/21 in sand with 100%-accurate Earthquake hitting both targets.
- Removing the 4x Ice weakness saves Mega Z less than it seems. M-Baxcalibur still OHKOs it (173-205.4%). Blizzard still KOs it on 8/16 rolls. Meanwhile, the physical Dragon hits from Garchomp and M-Salamence now OHKO it.
- Speed that lets you move first with half the damage, on a body that gets OHKO'd in return, loses to the form that wins the damage race across both slots.

Source note: all numbers are from factsheets/Garchomp.md, or from my runs of rotom-judge/engine.js on Jolly P(2,32,0,0,0,32).

### For Mega Garchomp Z
**Thesis:** Mega Garchomp Z is the stronger Reg M-C Mega. It is faster than all 21 threats on the board, it hits hardest where it needs to (special attacks that Intimidate cannot weaken), and it drops Mega Garchomp's 4x Ice weakness. The one thing Mega Garchomp has is Sand Force, and that only works if a teammate spends a slot setting sand.

**1. It moves first against the whole board (fact sheet).**
- Timid 32 Spe gives Mega Z 223 Speed.
- The sheet lists no Mega faster than that. Only Absol Mega Z and Lucario Mega Z tie it.
- Mega Garchomp tops out at 158. Six board threats outspeed it: Sneasler (34.29% usage), Whimsicott (184), M-Froslass (189), M-Staraptor (178), Garchomp (169), and M-Salamence, which ties at 158.
- Sneasler is the second most-used threat on the board and is faster than Mega Garchomp. Mega Z outspeeds it and has a 7/16 chance to OHKO it with Draco Meteor (90.4-107%).

**2. The strongest item-free offence on the sheet (fact sheet).**
- Both Megas hold their stone, so neither gets Life Orb or Choice Band. Base stats decide the damage.
- Timid Mega Z (193 SpA) OHKOs **7/21** with no item and no weather.
- Mega Garchomp OHKOs **6/21** on a neutral field.
- Mega Z's OHKOs include M-Salamence (138.7-164.5%), M-Baxcalibur (114.6-135%), M-Golisopod (Fire Blast 125.3-149.5%), Whimsicott (124.1-146%), Pelipper and Arcanine-Hisui.
- Mega Garchomp only OHKOs M-Salamence on 12/16 rolls and M-Baxcalibur on 6/16.

**3. Helping Hand doubles its KO list (my engine run, Timid 2/0/0/32/0/32).**
With Helping Hand, Mega Z OHKOs **14/21**. That adds:
- Sneasler: Draco Meteor 135.7-160.5%
- Archaludon: Draco Meteor 137.6-162.4%
- Gholdengo: Fire Blast 129.6-152.7%
- M-Froslass: Fire Blast 136.7-161.2%
- Kingambit: Fire Blast 110.1-130.4%
- M-Metagross: Fire Blast 108.8-128.1%
- M-Staraptor: Draco Meteor 104.3-123.8%

Because it outspeeds everything, the boosted hit lands before the target can act. Accuracy caveat (E.accuracy): Draco Meteor is 90% and Fire Blast is 85%.

**4. Intimidate does not weaken it.**
- Incineroar is the third most-used threat (26.77%) and brings Intimidate.
- Every Mega Garchomp number above drops at -1 Atk. Mega Z's special attacks are unaffected.

**5. Better defensive typing where it counts (fact sheet).**
- Mega Garchomp is 4x Ice-weak. Mega Z is only 2x.
- Milotic Ice Beam: 84.3-101.6% into Mega Garchomp, 46.5-55.1% into Mega Z.
- M-Froslass Blizzard: 164.3-196.8% (guaranteed KO) into Mega Garchomp, 90.8-108.1% (8/16) into Mega Z.
- Mega Z also resists Water, Electric and Grass.
- Levitate makes it immune to Ground. Opposing Earthquakes miss it, and it can partner a spread-Earthquake teammate without taking damage.
- Rillaboom (the most-used threat, 37.18%) hits Mega Z with Wood Hammer for only 44.3-51.9%. It does 68.1-80.5% to Mega Garchomp.

**Team role and partners**
Mega Z is a fast special cleaner that needs no weather. The best supports are:
- **Helping Hand user.** This turns 7 OHKOs into 14, as above.
- **Spread-Earthquake partner.** Its Earthquakes hit both opponents and pass harmlessly over Levitate.
- **Sun setter.** Sun boosts Fire Blast. In sun, Mega Z OHKOs Gholdengo (129-152.7%), Kingambit (110.1-130.4%) and M-Metagross (107.6-127.5%) without Helping Hand.

It needs no Tailwind or Trick Room. Mega Garchomp needs those just to move before Sneasler.

**Pre-empting the opponent: "Sand Force and more bulk"**
- **Sand costs a slot.** Mega Garchomp's best sheet number is 9/21 OHKOs in sand, but Garchomp does not set sand itself. A Tyranitar or Hippowdon has to lead with it. Weather set on entry resolves in Speed order and the last one written wins, so opposing Pelipper rain can overwrite that sand.
- **The out-of-sand advantage is small.** On a neutral field, Mega Garchomp's physical count is 6/21 against Mega Z's 7/21 special. Intimidate from the board's third most-used threat erodes its numbers further.
- **The bulk gap is one threat.** Mega Garchomp is OHKO'd by 4/21 and Mega Z by 5/21. Mega Garchomp's list includes guaranteed KOs from M-Froslass Blizzard and M-Baxcalibur because of its 4x Ice weakness, and M-Froslass outspeeds it. That is survival Mega Garchomp often does not get to use when the attacker moves first.

A Mega that outspeeds the whole board, OHKOs 14/21 with one Helping Hand, and ignores Intimidate is the better Mega.

## Round 2 — Rebuttals
### Mega Garchomp responds
**Rebuttal: Mega Garchomp**

**Concessions first.** Mega Z's Speed claim is correct. At 223 it outspeeds all 21 threats, and Mega Garchomp at 158 does not outspeed Sneasler, Whimsicott, M-Froslass, M-Staraptor or Garchomp, and only ties M-Salamence. Their Helping Hand numbers are also correct. I reran all seven additions and every one matches: Sneasler Draco Meteor 135.7-160.5%, Kingambit Fire Blast 110.1-130.4%, and so on. Intimidate also hurts me. At -1 in sand, spread Earthquake drops to 69.3-82.2% on Incineroar, 82.8-98.2% on Gholdengo and 54.1-65.2% on Kingambit, so none of those three is KO'd anymore. The 4x Ice weakness is real too.

**1. "14/21 with Helping Hand" means one KO per turn, with moves that miss.** Every one of those 14 KOs hits a single target. Seven of the Helping Hand additions use Fire Blast (85% accurate by E.accuracy) or Draco Meteor (90%). Draco Meteor also drops Special Attack by 2 after use, so Mega Z cannot repeat those KOs on the next turn. My Helping Hand sand Earthquake (100% accurate, x0.75 spread already applied) hits both slots. Even at -1 from Intimidate it still KOs Sneasler (193.6-231.2%), Incineroar (104.5-123.8%), Gholdengo (124.9-147.9%) and Arcanine-Hisui (285.5-340.1%). The opponent's own position depends on a Helping Hand partner, so "sand costs a slot" applies to them as well. The difference is that Hippowdon sets sand on entry and still gets to act. A Helping Hand partner gives up its whole turn.

**2. The Sneasler Speed argument overstates the result.** Without Helping Hand, Mega Z's answer to Sneasler is Draco Meteor at 90.4-107%. That KOs on 7/16 rolls and is 90% accurate, so it KOs about 39% of the time. Mega Garchomp moves second but OHKOs Sneasler with Earthquake in every condition: 150.3-177.1% on a neutral field and 129.3-154.1% in sand at -1. Sneasler's Close Combat does only 50.3-58.9% to Mega Garchomp, so Mega Garchomp survives the hit and KOs back. It takes 63.8-75.1% on Mega Z.

**3. "The bulk gap is one threat" hides which threats they are.** Mega Z is OHKO'd by Garchomp's Dragon Claw (110.8-131.9%) and by M-Salamence's Dragon Claw (100.5-120%). Those two threats have 17.12% and 23.15% usage. Against Mega Garchomp, M-Salamence does 78.9-94.1% and Garchomp KOs on only 3/16 rolls. Arcanine-Hisui's Head Smash KOs Mega Z on 11/16 rolls and does 36.8-43.8% to Mega Garchomp. Losing the Ice weakness does not save Mega Z much either: M-Baxcalibur still OHKOs it (173-205.4%) and M-Froslass's Blizzard still KOs it on 8/16 rolls. It trades Ice KOs for Dragon KOs from more common attackers.

**4. Their sun point costs them the same slot.** Fire Blast KOs on Gholdengo, Kingambit and M-Metagross need either sun or Helping Hand. Either way a partner is used up, the same cost they hold against sand.

**Conclusion:** Mega Z is faster, but its best turn is one 85-90% single-target KO that it cannot repeat. Mega Garchomp's best turn is a 100% accurate spread KO that still takes out four threats through Intimidate, and it survives the common Dragon attacks that OHKO Mega Z, so Mega Garchomp wins the damage race across both slots.

### Mega Garchomp Z responds
**Rebuttal for Mega Garchomp Z**

**1. The sand numbers leave out Intimidate from the third most-used threat.**
My opponent lists a sand Earthquake OHKO on Incineroar (104.5-123.8%). But Incineroar brings Intimidate every time it enters, so Mega Garchomp is normally attacking at -1. I ran spread Earthquake in sand at -1 on the engine:
- Incineroar: 69.3-82.2%, no KO.
- Gholdengo: 82.8-98.2%, no KO.
- Kingambit: 54.1-65.2%.

The "104.5-123.8%" figure on Incineroar is actually their **Helping Hand + sand + -1** result. Put another way, one Intimidate cancels the whole Helping Hand boost. Their 9/21 Helping Hand list assumes Incineroar never touched Garchomp's Attack. Mega Z's Draco Meteor and Fire Blast are special, so Intimidate does not change any of them.

**2. The spread plan needs three of the four slots, and the partners take the hit.**
The Helping Hand + sand board needs three things at once:
- a sand setter,
- a Helping Hand user,
- Garchomp itself.

Hippowdon cannot set sand and use Helping Hand in the same turn it has to be on the field. Also, Earthquake hits your own partner. Their own number shows it: 30.7-36.3% to Impish Hippowdon every turn. Rain can overwrite the sand too. Mega Z needs one partner (a Helping Hand user) to reach 14/21, with no weather involved. Levitate means a spread-Earthquake partner can fire freely next to it.

**3. Moving first decides who survives.**
My opponent says Mega "already OHKOs three of the six faster threats." Moving second makes that meaningless:
- **M-Froslass (189):** it moves first, and its Blizzard is a guaranteed KO on Mega (164.3-196.8%).
- **Whimsicott:** it sets Tailwind first.
- **Garchomp (169):** its Dragon Claw has a 3/16 chance to KO Mega first.

They cite physical Dragon hits that OHKO Mega Z:
- M-Salamence Dragon Claw: 100.5-120%.
- Garchomp Dragon Claw: 110.8-131.9%.

These only matter if the attacker moves first, and neither does:
- **Garchomp (169):** Mega Z (223) moves first and KOs it with Draco Meteor (145.9-173%, fact sheet).
- **M-Salamence (158):** Mega Z moves first and KOs it with Draco Meteor (138.7-164.5%).

Mega's own matchup with M-Salamence is a 158 Speed tie.

**Concessions.**
- Mega Garchomp has more physical bulk. Its 135 Def against Mega Z's 105 is real. Sneasler Close Combat (63.8-75.1%) and Arcanine-Hisui Head Smash (11/16 KO) hurt Mega Z more.
- Mega's Ground-STAB Earthquake does about twice Mega Z's physical Earthquake. That is true, but it doesn't matter much, because Mega Z runs special moves and would not click Earthquake.
- M-Baxcalibur still OHKOs Mega Z.
- My KO moves are less reliable than a 100%-accurate Earthquake: Draco Meteor is 90% accurate and Fire Blast is 85%.

**Mega Garchomp Z still wins.** It gets its damage from a single Helping Hand partner, Intimidate can't weaken it, and it moves before every threat on the board. Mega Garchomp's best numbers need sand, a Helping Hand user and no Intimidate all at once, and it often doesn't get to use its extra bulk because faster attackers hit it first.

## ⚖️ The Judge's Opinion
_Having heard both openings and both rebuttals, Rotom Judge rules on which form is better, and why._

Nearly every damage roll both sides cited reproduces.

**A's case.** A's Ground-STAB spread plan holds up under checking:
- With sand and Helping Hand, one 100%-accurate Earthquake OHKOs 9 board threats.
- Through Intimidate it still KOs 4 of them: Sneasler, Incineroar, Gholdengo and Arcanine-H.
- Mega Garchomp survives the physical hits that OHKO or nearly OHKO Mega Z: Garchomp and M-Salamence Dragon Claw, Arcanine-H Head Smash and Sneasler Close Combat.

**A's key rebuttal point also holds.** Mega Z's KOs are single-target and 85-90% accurate, and Draco Meteor cannot be repeated. At -2 SpA it does only 44.6-53.5% to Sneasler.

**B's "three slots" argument also fails, for a reason B missed.** Hippowdon learns Helping Hand. It sets sand on entry and uses Helping Hand in the same turn, so the full sand + Helping Hand setup needs two slots, not three.

**B's case.** B's real, proven points are these:
- 223 Speed outspeeds the whole board.
- Special attacks ignore Intimidate. At -1 in sand without Helping Hand, Mega's EQ loses the Incineroar, Gholdengo and Kingambit KOs.
- Removing the 4x Ice weakness is real: Milotic Ice Beam 46.5-55.1% and snow Icy Wind 45.4-55.1%.

But B's headline counts are inflated by the two Focus Sash holders: 7/21 is really 5, and 14/21 is really 12. B also misread A's Incineroar number.

**What neither side raised, and it cuts both ways:**
- **Grassy Terrain from Rillaboom (37.18%, the #1 threat) halves Earthquake on grounded targets.** Sand EQ drops to 52-61.9% on Incineroar, 62.1-74% on Gholdengo and 41.5-48.8% on Kingambit. It still KOs Sneasler on 13/16 rolls. A Rillaboom + Incineroar lead is the worst matchup for Mega Garchomp.
- **Rain from Pelipper (13.09%) halves Fire Blast.** HH Fire Blast on Gholdengo drops to 63.9-76.3%. It also overwrites sand on any later switch-in.
- **Sneasler (34.29%) holds a Psychic Seed.** In Indeedee-F's Psychic Terrain it gets +1 SpD, which drops Mega Z's Draco Meteor to 59.9-71.3%. Unburden then doubles its Speed. That is a standard mechanic the engine does not model, and it would put Sneasler above 223.

**Verdict.** On what was proven, Mega Garchomp is the stronger Mega for a team built around sand. Mega Z is a real alternative for a weather-free, fast special attacker.

### 🏆 Conclusion
**Rotom Judge rules that Mega Garchomp is the better form** (Mega Garchomp 75, Mega Garchomp Z 68).

Mega Garchomp wins narrowly, and only on a team built around sand.

Its Ground-STAB spread Earthquake is 100% accurate and hits both slots. With sand and Helping Hand it OHKOs 9 of the 21 board threats. Through Intimidate it still KOs Sneasler, Incineroar, Gholdengo and Arcanine-H. It also takes the physical Dragon Claws that OHKO Mega Z (Garchomp 110.8-131.9%, M-Salamence 100.5-120% on Z).

B's objection that the plan needs three slots fails because Hippowdon learns Helping Hand. It sets sand and uses Helping Hand on the same turn.

Mega Z's proven strengths are real:
- At 223 Speed it outspeeds the whole board.
- Its special attacks ignore Intimidate.
- It is only 2x Ice-weak, not 4x.

But its headline counts are inflated by the two Focus Sash holders: 7/21 is really 5, and 14/21 is really 12. Every KO is single-target at 85-90% accuracy, and Draco Meteor at -2 SpA does only 44.6-53.5% to Sneasler.

Neither side raised the biggest threat to Mega's plan. Rillaboom (37.18% usage) sets Grassy Terrain, which halves Earthquake: sand EQ on Incineroar drops to 52-61.9%. Mega Z's matching weakness is rain, which halves Fire Blast.

Jolly 2/32/32 Mega Garchomp spams 100%-accurate Earthquake for turn-one double KOs. Surround it with Levitate/Flying partners so EQ is free.
