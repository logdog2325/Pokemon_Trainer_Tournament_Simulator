# Rotom Judge: Mega Lucario vs Mega Lucario Z

**The ruling:** Rotom Judge ruled that **Mega Lucario** is the better form. Final score: Mega Lucario 77, Mega Lucario Z 80 (out of 100).

## What this is
Rotom Judge is an experiment in settling a Pokémon argument with evidence. Some Pokémon have two competing
super-forms (two Mega Evolutions, or a Mega against a special battle form), and players argue about which is better. For
each one, two AI advocates each argued for one form. They were not allowed to rely on memory: every claim had to come from a damage calculator
built for Pokémon Champions, and they could run new calculations. Each advocate gave an opening argument, then read the
other side's opening and wrote a rebuttal. An impartial AI judge then recomputed the disputed numbers itself, scored
both sides, wrote its opinion and ruled on which Mega is better. Finally it built the strongest competitive team it
could around each form.

## The format
- **Pokémon Champions, Regulation M-C**: the official competitive (VGC) ruleset running September to December 2026.
- **Doubles**: two Pokémon on each side at once. Each player brings six Pokémon and picks four for each game. Level 50.
- **Mega Evolution**: a Pokémon holding its Mega Stone can transform once per battle into a stronger Mega form. Only one
  Pokémon per team can Mega Evolve per battle. **Z Megas** (Mega Absol Z, Mega Garchomp Z, Mega Lucario Z) are new
  alternate Mega forms added in Regulation M-C.
- **Stat points**: each Pokémon gets 66 points to spread across its six stats, at most 32 in any one stat.
- **Item Clause and Species Clause**: all six items on a team must be different, and so must all six species.

## How to read the numbers
- **Damage ranges** like "99.5-118%" are the percentage of the target's HP one attack removes. Pokémon damage has 16
  random rolls, so every attack has a lowest and highest outcome.
- **"(14/16)"** means 14 of the 16 possible rolls knock the target out. **"KO"** alone means all 16 do.
- **OHKO** means a one-hit knockout. **2HKO** means it takes two hits.
- **Spread moves** hit both opponents at once but deal 25% less damage in doubles.
- **STAB** (same-type attack bonus): a Pokémon's attacks of its own type do 1.5x damage.
- **Speed** decides who moves first. Moves with **priority** (Sucker Punch, Fake Out, First Impression) go first anyway.
- **Usage %** is how often a Pokémon appears on ranked teams (Pikalytics, Regulation M-C). The debates focus on the
  21 most important threats, led by Rillaboom (37%), Sneasler (34%), Incineroar (27%), Mega Salamence (23%) and
  Kingambit (22%).

## Terms that come up a lot
- **Intimidate**: an ability that lowers both opposing Pokémon's Attack by one stage when it enters. It weakens
  physical attackers only.
- **Psychic Terrain**: a field effect that boosts Psychic moves and blocks priority moves aimed at Pokémon that are on
  the ground. Flying types and Pokémon with Levitate are not on the ground, so they are not protected.
- **Tailwind / Trick Room**: speed control. Tailwind doubles your side's Speed; Trick Room makes slower Pokémon move first.
- **Helping Hand**: a support move that makes a partner's attack 50% stronger that turn.
- **Follow Me**: a support move that pulls opposing single-target attacks onto the user.
- **Sun, rain, sand, snow**: weather. Sun boosts Fire moves, rain boosts Water moves; some abilities only work in one weather.

## Round 1: Opening arguments
### Opening argument for Mega Lucario (Advocate A)
**Thesis:** Adaptability gives Mega Lucario (holding Lucarionite) the strongest hits either Lucario can throw, on either attacking stat. It removes most of the Reg M-C board with accurate moves, while Mega Lucario Z depends on 70%-accurate Focus Blast and fixes only half the problem with its bulk.

Mega Stones take the item slot, so every number below is a no-item calc. Life Orb and Choice Band are not available to either form.

**1. More OHKOs, and they don't depend on luck (fact sheet).**
- Mega's Jolly physical set OHKOs **12/21** board threats. The Z form's best set OHKOs **9/21**.
- The Z form's line is built on Focus Blast into Rillaboom, Incineroar, Kingambit, Garchomp and more, and Focus Blast hits 70% of the time.
- Mega's OHKOs come from accurate moves (engine run):
  - Close Combat (100%): Incineroar 152.5-180.2%, Kingambit 121.7-144.9%, Archaludon 132.6-156.9%, Arcanine-Hisui 209.3-246.5%, M-Baxcalibur 126.2-149.5%, M-Tyranitar 201-239.6%.
  - Ice Punch: Garchomp 105.9-125.4%.
  - Thunder Punch: Pelipper 137.2-163.5%.
- That is 8 sure OHKOs, with no accuracy discount, against 5 of the most-used threats on the board.

**2. Adaptability beats the Z form's 24 extra SpA on the Z form's own special game (engine).**
- On Timid 32 SpA, Mega Aura Sphere does 108.2-127.5% to Kingambit, a guaranteed OHKO. The Z form's Aura Sphere does 89.9-107.2% (6/16).
- Into Incineroar, Mega does 83.2-99% and the Z form does 71.3-84.2%.
- Mega Flash Cannon does 88.1-106.2% to Sylveon. The Z form does 74.6-88.1%.
- Mega Vacuum Wave does 85-100.5% to M-Tyranitar. The Z form does 69.6-83.1%.
- The Z form's high Special Attack does not make up for losing the x2 STAB.

**3. Priority with double STAB covers the three threats that outspeed it (sheet plus engine).**
- At 180 Speed, Mega is outspeeded only by Sneasler (189), Whimsicott (184) and M-Froslass (189).
- Meteor Mash OHKOs all three: Sneasler 107-126.1%, Whimsicott 186.9-221.9%, Froslass 201.4-239.5%. It is 90% accurate.
- Bullet Punch, a priority move at +1, does 92.5-108.8% to M-Froslass and 84.7-102.2% to Whimsicott before they move.

**4. Helping Hand turns the top threat into an OHKO (engine).**
- With Helping Hand, High Jump Kick does 124.6-147.8% to Rillaboom (37.18% usage) and Close Combat does 114.5-136.2%. Unboosted, the best hit is 83.1-98.6%.
- Helping Hand Close Combat also OHKOs Sneasler (107-126.1%), Indeedee-F (118.6-140.7%), Farigiraf (106.8-127%) and Milotic (121.8-144.1%).
- Those are the first- and sixth-most-used threats, plus Farigiraf and Milotic, all removed in one move.

**5. Swords Dance takes over the game (engine, +2 Atk).**
- At +2, Close Combat OHKOs Rillaboom (152.7-180.7%), M-Metagross (122.8-145%), Pelipper (108-127.7%) and Milotic (161.4-190.1%). M-Golisopod goes down on 15/16 rolls (98.9-117.6%).
- At +2, Meteor Mash OHKOs M-Staraptor (121.1-142.7%).
- On this board, only Gholdengo (68.6-81.1%) and M-Salamence (96.8-114%, 13/16) avoid a sure OHKO. Ice Punch OHKOs M-Salamence at 159.1-189.2%.
- The Z form's Aura Guard gives it nothing comparable.

**Team role and partners.**
- Mega Lucario is a Jolly 2/32/32 physical sweeper with Close Combat, Meteor Mash, Ice Punch or Bullet Punch, and Protect or Swords Dance.
- It pairs best with a Helping Hand or redirection partner. Indeedee-F's Follow Me covers a Swords Dance turn. Incineroar's Fake Out and Intimidate help it survive the fire and fighting hits it fears.
- A Tailwind setter such as Whimsicott lifts it to 360 Speed, above the entire board.
- Lucario resists Normal, Grass, Ice, Dragon, Dark and Steel, and is immune to Poison. That lets it switch into Kingambit, Rillaboom's Grassy Glide and Garchomp's Dragon Claw.

**Pre-empting the Z form's case: 223 Speed and Aura Guard.**
- **Speed.** Outspeeding all 21 threats only matters if the hit that follows is a KO. Against Rillaboom, Incineroar, Kingambit and Garchomp, the Z form's KO or near-KO move is Focus Blast, which fails 30% of the time. On a two-target turn, both Focus Blasts land only 49% of the time.
- **Bulk.** The Z form is OHKO'd by 4/21 threats against Mega's 8/21. Aura Guard only halves contact moves, though, and the Z form's Defense is 90 against Mega's 108:
  - Garchomp's Earthquake OHKOs the Z form harder: 148.3-176.9%, against 127.2-150.3% on Mega.
  - Sylveon's Hyper Beam, Pelipper's Weather Ball and Gholdengo's Shadow Ball do exactly the same to both forms.
  - The Z form still takes 92.5-110.2% from Sneasler's Close Combat (10/16 OHKO).
- **Summary.** The Z form survives more hits but deals less damage with each attack. Mega Lucario turns each hit it takes into an accurate OHKO.

### Opening argument for Mega Lucario Z (Advocate B)
**Opening argument for Mega Lucario Z**

**Thesis:** Mega Lucario Z is the better Mega for Reg M-C doubles. It is the fastest Mega in the format, half as many board threats can OHKO it, and it doesn't depend on a 90%-accurate crash move or on an Attack stat that Intimidate can cut.

**1. Nothing on the board outspeeds it (fact sheet).** Timid 32 Spe gives Mega Z 223 Speed. It outspeeds all 21 board threats, and no Mega is faster; it only ties Absol Mega Z and Garchomp Mega Z. Mega Lucario tops out at 180. Sneasler (189, 34.29% usage, the #2 threat), Whimsicott (184) and M-Froslass (189) all move before it, and 22 Megas are faster than it. The Sneasler matchup decides the argument. Without Psychic Terrain (its Psychic Seed Unburden needs terrain), Mega Z moves first and Psychic does 186–221.7%, a KO. Mega Lucario moves second and takes Close Combat for 155.1–183.7%, a KO. Mega Z also survives that Close Combat in 6 of 16 rolls (92.5–110.2%).

**2. Aura Guard halves the board's main attacks (fact sheet).** Mega Z is OHKO'd by 4 of 21 threats; Mega Lucario by 8 of 21. The threats Mega Lucario loses to but Mega Z survives:
- Incineroar Flare Blitz (26.77% usage): 126.5–148.3% KO becomes 75.5–89.8%
- M-Salamence Double-Edge: 110.2–130.6% KO becomes 66.0–78.2%
- M-Golisopod Close Combat: 164.6–194.6% KO becomes 99.3–117%
- M-Staraptor Close Combat: 144.2–171.4% KO becomes 86.4–102.7% (only 3/16 KO)
- Kingambit Low Kick: 77.6–92.5% becomes 46.9–55.8%

My engine run with a 32 HP / 2 Def / 32 Spe spread (HP 177) stops every contact move on the board from OHKOing it:
- Sneasler Close Combat: 76.3–89.8%
- Golisopod Close Combat: 80.2–94.9%
- Arcanine-Hisui Flare Blitz: 90.4–106.2%, only 7/16 KO

It keeps 223 Speed on that spread.

**3. Intimidate doesn't touch it (my engine run).** Incineroar is the #3 threat at 26.77% usage and brings Intimidate. Mega Lucario's damage depends on its Attack stat. At −1, its High Jump Kick on Kingambit drops from a clean KO (131.4–156.5%) to 87–104.3% (4/16). Its HJK on Rillaboom drops to 55.1–65.7%, and on Garchomp to 60.5–72.4%. Mega Z attacks with 216 SpA: Aura Sphere, Flash Cannon, Psychic and Vacuum Wave. Intimidate does nothing to any of them.

**4. Its attacks hit reliably (my engine run, E.accuracy).** Aura Sphere is 100% accurate. It KOs M-Tyranitar (135.3–162.3%), Archaludon (127.1–150.3%) and Arcanine-Hisui (115.1–136%). Flash Cannon KOs M-Froslass (111.6–132%), and Psychic KOs Sneasler. Mega Lucario's board-wide numbers lean on High Jump Kick (90%, and it crashes on a miss) and Iron Tail (75%). Its answers to Whimsicott, Sylveon, M-Froslass and M-Staraptor are all Iron Tail, so a quarter of those turns do nothing.

**5. Helping Hand adds KOs (my engine run).** With Helping Hand, Aura Sphere KOs Incineroar (106.9–126.2%), Kingambit (134.8–160.9%) and M-Baxcalibur (118–139.8%). It also puts Garchomp at 75.1–89.7% before Garchomp can act. Vacuum Wave gives it Fighting priority to finish targets: 69.6–83.1% on M-Tyranitar and 64.1–76.2% on Archaludon.

**Team role and partners.** Mega Z is a speed-control-independent lead. It outspeeds Sneasler and M-Froslass without Tailwind, which frees a team slot. Good partners:
- **Helping Hand user**, to reach the KOs above.
- **Fake Out user**, to cover it.
- **Ground-immune or Wide Guard partner.** Garchomp Earthquake is its one clean loss (148.3–176.9% KO).
- **A way to deny Indeedee-F's Psychic Terrain.** That terrain is the only thing that lets a Psychic Seed Sneasler outspeed it.

Because the Mega holds its stone, it can't use Choice Band or Specs anyway (neither exists in Champions). So Mega Lucario's item headroom doesn't exist, and the forms compare on raw stats and ability.

**Pre-empting the opponent.** Mega Lucario's best point is Adaptability's 12/21 no-item OHKOs against Mega Z's 9/21. The raw count overstates it:
- It is a Speed-180 Pokémon, and Sneasler, Whimsicott and M-Froslass all move before it. Mega Lucario is OHKO'd by 8 board threats, including the #2 (Sneasler) and #3 (Incineroar).
- Several of those OHKOs depend on 90% HJK or 75% Iron Tail.
- The first Intimidate from the #3-usage threat turns KOs into 2HKOs. Kingambit goes from a KO to 87–104.3%.

Damage that loses the Speed race, can miss, and shrinks when Incineroar switches in is not as good as the table makes it look.

On Fake Out: the engine prices Incineroar's Fake Out at 8.2–10.2% on Mega Z against 6.8–8.2% on Mega Lucario, so it doesn't appear to apply Aura Guard to Fake Out. `MF_CONTACT` in `champions-team-building/app/app.js` may be missing Fake Out. I left it out of the argument.

  - Mega Lucario Z's only way to KO either one is Focus Blast. The engine gives it 70% accuracy.
- **Mega Lucario outspeeds and OHKOs most of what Mega Lucario Z mainly outspeeds.** At 180 Speed, only three threats are faster than Mega Lucario (Sneasler, Whimsicott and M-Froslass). Meteor Mash OHKOs Whimsicott and M-Froslass. Bullet Punch puts real damage on both before they move (84.7–102.2% on Whimsicott, 92.5–108.8% on M-Froslass).

**Summary.** Mega Lucario Z wins the Sneasler matchup and takes contact hits better. Mega Lucario hits harder on either stat, and many of its KOs survive Intimidate at −1. Its KOs come from 100% and 90% moves, not from 70% Focus Blast. In a format decided by removing threats, that matters more than an extra 43 Speed.

**Why Mega Lucario still wins:** Mega Lucario Z has to choose between bulk and damage and still needs a 70% Focus Blast for its biggest KOs, while Mega Lucario turns each turn into an accurate KO on either attacking stat.

## The judge's opinion
Having heard both openings and both rebuttals, Rotom Judge wrote this opinion.

Almost every damage roll reproduces. The debate turned on three things: Focus Sash, Speed, and how many accurate OHKOs each form actually has.

**Accurate OHKOs decide it.** This count uses moves with at least 90% accuracy, ignores Focus Sash holders and uses no item.
- **Mega Lucario (Jolly)** OHKOs 10 threats: Sneasler (Zen Headbutt), Incineroar, Kingambit, Archaludon, Arcanine-H, M-Baxcalibur and M-Tyranitar (all Close Combat), Garchomp (Ice Punch), and Sylveon and M-Froslass (Meteor Mash). That is 130.61% of summed usage.
- **Mega Lucario Z (Timid)** OHKOs 5: Sneasler (Psychic), Archaludon, Arcanine-H and M-Tyranitar (Aura Sphere), and M-Froslass (Flash Cannon). That is 53.65% of summed usage.
- B conceded that without Focus Blast, Rillaboom, Incineroar and Garchomp are 2HKOs for Z. Rillaboom takes 35.3-42% from Aura Sphere, and Garchomp takes 50.3-60%.

**B's real wins:**
- **Sneasler.** Z moves first and Psychic KOs it (186-221.7%). Mega is outsped and OHKO'd by Close Combat (155.1-183.7%). This is a clean win for Z against the #2 threat.
- **Aura Guard.** Z is OHKO'd by 4/21 threats against Mega's 8/21. Incineroar's Flare Blitz, M-Salamence's Double-Edge and M-Staraptor's Close Combat stop being KOs.
- **Intimidate.** It costs the physical Mega its Kingambit KO (81.2-96.6%), its Garchomp KO (Ice Punch 71.4-84.3%) and its Helping Hand Rillaboom KOs.

**Why Mega still wins:**
- At -1, Mega's Close Combat still OHKOs Incineroar itself (101-120.8%), and Meteor Mash still OHKOs M-Froslass.
- A Timid Mega out-damages Z's best special set on every shared target and ignores Intimidate just as Z does. Aura Sphere does 108.2-127.5% to Kingambit against Z's 89.9-107.2%.
- Z's defensive edge does not cover Garchomp. Earthquake hits Z harder: 148.3-176.9% against Mega's 127.2-150.3%.
- Z cannot take both bulk and damage. A's rebuttal proved this, and B conceded it.

**A's overclaims.** Two of A's OHKOs are blocked by Focus Sash: Pelipper (Thunder Punch) and Whimsicott (Meteor Mash). A also offered Helping Hand Close Combat on Sneasler as a KO even though Sneasler moves first. Bullet Punch is also blocked by Indeedee-F's Psychic Terrain against grounded targets.

**Engine gap (not scored against either side).** Aura Guard is not applied to Fake Out or Glaive Rush, because both are missing from `MF_CONTACT`. The real damage Z takes from those two moves is about half what is shown.

### Conclusion
Rotom Judge rules that Mega Lucario is the better form (Mega Lucario 77, Mega Lucario Z 80).

Mega Lucario wins on evidence, even though Mega Lucario Z's advocate argued more cleanly and scores slightly higher (80 vs 77).

- **Accurate OHKOs decide it.** This counts moves with at least 90% accuracy, no item, and leaves out the Focus Sash holders (Pelipper, Whimsicott). Mega OHKOs 10 threats (130.61% summed usage), including Incineroar, Kingambit and Garchomp. Z OHKOs 5 (53.65%). B conceded that Rillaboom, Incineroar and Garchomp are 2HKOs for Z without 70% Focus Blast.
- **Z's real wins.** It moves first against Sneasler and KOs it with Psychic (186-221.7%), while Mega is outsped and OHKO'd by Close Combat (155.1-183.7%). Aura Guard cuts Z's OHKO count to 4/21 against Mega's 8/21. Its special damage ignores Intimidate.
- **Intimidate costs Mega some KOs, not all.** At -1, Mega loses its Kingambit and Garchomp KOs but still OHKOs Incineroar (101-120.8%). A Timid Mega out-damages Z on every shared special target.
