## Ruling

Nearly every damage roll both sides cited reproduces. The debate turned on which premises survived.

A's opening depended on a Life Orb that Mega Absol cannot hold alongside Absolite. Without it, the claim that A OHKOs more of the board reverses. On legal items, with 100%-accurate moves only and not counting Focus Sash holders, Z OHKOs **7** threats (96.58% usage) and Mega OHKOs **5** (58.90%).

A's defensive argument was built on the fact sheet's Jolly spread. A then recommended a Hasty spread that drops Defense to 72. On that spread, Mega is OHKO'd by **16 of 21** threats, not 13. Kingambit's Iron Head, Incineroar's Flare Blitz and Garchomp's Dragon Claw all become KOs.

A's Magic Bounce case also shrinks. Under the standard Prankster-vs-Dark rule, Whimsicott's Encore already fails against both Absol forms. That leaves Incineroar's Parting Shot as the main status move that Magic Bounce uniquely stops on this board.

A's real, proven points are these:
- 100%-accurate Ice Beam is the only answer to Garchomp and M-Salamence that either form has. It lands 12/16 and 8/16 unboosted and guarantees both KOs with Helping Hand.
- Intimidate takes a third off every one of Z's kills. B's Helping Hand and +1 KOs all fall short at -1.

B's case held up. Z moves first against the whole board. It is immune to both Fake Out users and to the Fighting moves of Sneasler (34.29%) and M-Staraptor. It is OHKO'd by 12 of 21 threats against Mega's 13-16, and it OHKOs more of the board without needing an item.

B's weak points:
- The Pelipper Helping Hand KO ignores Focus Sash.
- Phantom Force is two-turn, so it is not a real lead KO.
- B conceded, correctly, that Intimidate hurts Z.

Z is the better form. Its main liabilities are Intimidate, Fairy hits and Garchomp/Salamence, and each can be fixed with a partner. Mega's Fighting/Bug weakness and low Defense cannot be fixed that way.

## Team brief: Mega Absol Z (Absolite Z), the winner

**Game plan:** Z is the fastest Pokemon on the board: Jolly 32 Spe gives 223, and it outspeeds all 21 threats. It leads as a Sharpness sweeper. Set: Night Slash / X-Scissor / Sucker Punch or Shadow Sneak / Protect, with Swords Dance as an option. It ignores Fake Out and Fighting moves, and a Helping Hand partner turns its 2HKOs into OHKOs. Keep Intimidate off it, and remove Fairy attackers and the Dragons with partners.

**Lean into**
- Speed 223: it moves before Sneasler (189), Whimsicott (184), M-Froslass (189) and M-Staraptor (178). It only ties Garchomp Z and Lucario Z.
- Immune to Fake Out (Rillaboom, Incineroar) and to Close Combat from Sneasler and M-Staraptor. Sneasler's best hit is Dire Claw at 35.9-43%.
- No-item OHKOs: Night Slash on Indeedee-F 108.5-128.8%, on Gholdengo 127.8-150.9%, on Farigiraf 98.6-117.6% (14/16), on M-Froslass 185.7-220.4%. Psycho Cut on Sneasler 259.9-309.6%. Close Combat on Arcanine-H 108.1-127.9% and M-Tyranitar 106.3-125.6%.
- Helping Hand: X-Scissor on Rillaboom 121.7-143.5% KO, Night Slash on M-Metagross 128.7-152.6% KO, Play Rough on Garchomp 98.9-116.8% (14/16).
- +2 after Swords Dance, Night Slash OHKOs Rillaboom 104.8-124.6%, Garchomp 115.1-136.2% and Milotic 111.4-131.2%. At +1, X-Scissor OHKOs Rillaboom 121.7-143.5%.

**Must cover**
- **Intimidate** (Incineroar, 26.77%). At -1, Night Slash on Gholdengo drops to 85.2-101.2% (1/16), Indeedee-F to 71.2-86.4% and Farigiraf to 66.2-78.4%. HH X-Scissor on Rillaboom drops to 82.6-97.6%. There is no Clear Amulet in Champions. Also Incineroar's Parting Shot lands on Z. Needs a partner that KOs or pressures Incineroar, or a Defiant/Competitive teammate to punish it.
- **Fairy**: Whimsicott's Moonblast does 122.5-145.1% (KO) and Sylveon's Hyper Beam 354.9-419.7%. Needs a Steel or Poison partner, or something that removes them first. Whimsicott has a Sash, so Z alone cannot OHKO it.
- **M-Golisopod First Impression** at 128.9-152.1%. It is priority, so Z's Speed does not help. Needs a Psychic Terrain partner (Z is grounded) or a Wide Guard/Protect read.
- **M-Salamence and Garchomp**: Z cannot OHKO either one. Stone Edge on Salamence does 55.9-66.7% (80% accuracy), 83.9-100% with Helping Hand. Play Rough on Garchomp does 65.9-77.8% (90%). M-Salamence's Double-Edge does 154.9-182.4% to Z. Needs an Ice-type attacker or a Fairy partner.
- **Other OHKOs taken**: Rillaboom's Wood Hammer 150.7-178.2%, M-Staraptor's Brave Bird 101.4-119%, Kingambit's Kowtow Cleave 86.6-102.1% (3/16), Kingambit's Sucker Punch 71.8-84.5%.
- **Poor targets**: Kingambit (Night Slash 20.8-24.6%, Close Combat 63.8-75.4% into its Chople Berry) and Incineroar. Leave both to a partner.

**Partner ideas**
- A Helping Hand user, ideally one that also brings Fairy or Ice coverage for the Dragons.
- A Steel-type Fairy answer, such as Gholdengo, Archaludon or Kingambit, for Whimsicott and Sylveon. Kingambit also takes Dark hits.
- An Incineroar answer, such as a Fighting- or Water-type attacker, or Intimidate of your own to blunt Rillaboom, Salamence and Golisopod.
- Optional Indeedee-F for Psychic Terrain against First Impression and Sucker Punch, with Follow Me. But Psychic Terrain also triggers an opposing Sneasler's Psychic Seed, and Z's own Sucker Punch and Shadow Sneak fail into grounded targets under it.
- No Tailwind is needed. Trick Room is anti-synergy.

## Team brief: Mega Absol (Absolite)

**Game plan:** A fast mixed attacker (183 Spe) whose job is to fire 100%-accurate special coverage that Intimidate cannot touch, with Magic Bounce blocking Parting Shot. Set: Ice Beam / Foul Play / Close Combat or Thunderbolt / Protect. Run it next to Helping Hand and a redirector, because it is extremely frail: on the Hasty spread it is OHKO'd by 16 of 21 threats. Use a Jolly spread, or add Def points, if the team cannot shield it.

**Lean into**
- Ice Beam, 100% accurate and unaffected by Intimidate: Garchomp 97.3-114.6% (12/16), M-Salamence 92.5-109.7% (8/16). With Helping Hand, both are guaranteed KOs (145.9-171.9% and 138.7-164.5%).
- Foul Play uses the target's Attack: Gholdengo 111.2-132.5% KO, M-Froslass 163.3-193.2% KO, Indeedee-F 94.9-113% (11/16), Farigiraf 87.4-103.6% (4/16).
- Other KOs: Zen Headbutt on Sneasler 193.6-229.3%, Close Combat on Arcanine-H 105.8-125.6% and M-Tyranitar 104.3-123.7%. Thunderbolt does 151.8-181% to Pelipper but only breaks its Sash.
- Resists Dark and Ghost: Kowtow Cleave 48.6-57.9%, Sucker Punch 40-47.1%, Foul Play 17.9-20.7% (Hasty).
- Magic Bounce reflects Incineroar's Parting Shot. Do not count on it against Whimsicott's Encore, because Prankster already fails on Dark types.

**Must cover**
- **Fighting and Bug** (Hasty spread): Sneasler's Close Combat 244.3-288.6%, and Sneasler at 189 outspeeds Mega. M-Staraptor's Close Combat 227.1-267.1% and Brave Bird 113.6-133.6%. M-Golisopod's First Impression 291.4-345.7% (priority).
- **Fake Out** (Rillaboom 29.3-35%, Incineroar 22.1-26.4%). Magic Bounce does not block it.
- **Physical fragility on Hasty (72 Def)**: Kingambit's Iron Head 92.1-109.3% (8/16), Incineroar's Flare Blitz 98.6-116.4% (14/16), Garchomp's Dragon Claw 105.7-125%, M-Tyranitar's Low Kick 105.7-125.7%, Rillaboom's Wood Hammer 170-200.7%.
- **Fairy**: Whimsicott's Moonblast 124.3-147.1%, and Whimsicott (184) is also faster.
- **Rillaboom (37.18%)**: Mega's best answer is Megahorn at 79.2-93.7% (85% accuracy). Fire Blast only does 41.5-49.3% through Assault Vest.
- Only 5 honest no-item OHKOs (58.90% usage) once Sash holders and inaccurate moves are removed.

**Partner ideas**
- Indeedee-F: Follow Me for Sneasler and Staraptor, and Psychic Terrain against Fake Out, Sucker Punch and First Impression (Mega is grounded). Watch out: the terrain triggers an opposing Sneasler's Psychic Seed (Unburden).
- A Helping Hand user, to turn Ice Beam into guaranteed Garchomp and Salamence KOs.
- Intimidate support (Incineroar) against Sneasler, Golisopod, Rillaboom and Staraptor.
- A Steel or Poison partner against Whimsicott and Sylveon.
- A Wide Guard or Fighting-resist partner, such as a Psychic or Flying type, to absorb Close Combat.
