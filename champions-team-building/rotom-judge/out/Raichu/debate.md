# Rotom Judge: Mega Raichu X vs Mega Raichu Y

> **Winner: Mega Raichu Y** — Mega Raichu X **63** / Mega Raichu Y **79** (out of 100)

| | Mega Raichu X | Mega Raichu Y |
|---|---|---|
| Total | 63 | 79 |
| Evidence / Relevance / Rebuttal / Honesty | 21/40, 20/25, 14/20, 8/15 | 33/40, 20/25, 15/20, 11/15 |

Rubric: Evidence 40 (minus 5 per checked claim that fails), Relevance to the Reg M-C board 25, Rebuttal 20, Honesty 15.

---
## Round 1 — Opening arguments
### For Mega Raichu X
**Thesis:** Mega Raichu X is the better Mega for Reg M-C doubles. It is the only Raichu form that changes the field state the most-used teams depend on, and it lives through hits that knock out Mega Y before it can act a second time.

**1. It takes away the two most common terrains on turn 1.** Rillaboom is the #1 threat on the board at 37.18% usage and Indeedee-F is at 21.78%. Both are Surge setters. Mega Evolution resolves at the start of the turn, after lead switch-in abilities have fired (confirmed-facts.md, rule 2). So Electric Surge writes last, and it replaces Grassy or Psychic Terrain before anyone moves. It fires again every time X switches back in. Mega Y has no answer to terrain at all. From my own engine runs on the fact-sheet spreads:
- Indeedee-F's Expanding Force on X falls from 48.2-56.9% in Psychic Terrain to 33.6-40.1% under X's Electric Terrain. It also stops being a spread move.
- Rillaboom's Wood Hammer on X falls from 108.8-129.2% (a KO) in Grassy Terrain to 83.9-99.3% (0/16 KO rolls) in Electric Terrain.
- Grassy Glide loses its priority.
- Psychic Terrain would block X's own Fake Out against grounded targets. Removing it gives the move back.

**2. It is about twice as hard to knock out.** From the fact sheet, on the same no-investment spread:
- 5 of 21 board threats OHKO X; 11 of 21 OHKO Y.
- Y is OHKO'd by Sneasler Close Combat (120.4-142.3%), Kingambit Kowtow Cleave (12/16), Incineroar Flare Blitz (13/16) and M-Salamence Dragon Claw (11/16).
- X takes 78.8-92.7%, 62-74.5%, 63.5-75.2% and 62-74.5% from those same hits.

X has base 95/95 defences against Y's 55/80. Raichu has only the one Mega slot, and a Mega that dies before its second action wastes it.

**3. Bulk investment actually works on X.** On Adamant 32 HP / 32 Atk / 2 Def (167 HP / 117 Def), my engine runs show:
- Garchomp's spread Earthquake still KOs (103-121.6%), so the Ground weakness stays a matter for positioning.
- Life Orb Garchomp's spread Earthquake does 103-121.6%, so the Ground weakness needs positioning.
- Mega Golisopod's First Impression: 75.4-89.2%.
- Sneasler's Close Combat: 63.5-75.4%.
- Grassy Wood Hammer: 87.4-103.6% (only 4/16 KO rolls).

**4. It hits hard, and grounded targets take its terrain boost.** From the fact sheet, with no item, X OHKOs 6 of 21 board threats and Y OHKOs 5. With Life Orb, X OHKOs 9 of 21 (my engine run), including:
- Sneasler (171.3-201.3%)
- Gholdengo (108.9-130.2%)
- Sylveon (112.4-134.5%)
- Milotic, Pelipper, Arcanine-Hisui, M-Froslass and M-Staraptor
- Whimsicott, via Iron Tail at 75% accuracy (discount that one)

**5. Helping Hand turns the defensive core of the format into KOs.** My engine runs for Life Orb Volt Tackle with Helping Hand in its own terrain:
- Kingambit: 110.1-130%, KO
- M-Salamence: 114.5-136%, KO
- M-Metagross: 112.9-133.3%, KO
- Farigiraf: 130.2-154.1%, KO
- Indeedee-F: 142.9-168.9%, KO

Even at -1 from Intimidate, it still KOs Indeedee-F in 12/16 rolls and Incineroar in 7/16. The two Trick Room setters on the board can't sit in front of it.

**6. It outspeeds almost all of the usage-weighted board.** At Jolly 32 Spe it has 178 Speed. That beats 17 of 21 threats, including Garchomp (169), M-Salamence (158), Gholdengo (144) and Arcanine-Hisui (156). Only Sneasler, Whimsicott, M-Froslass and M-Staraptor are faster, and it KOs Sneasler, M-Froslass and M-Staraptor outright (fact sheet).

**Team role and partners.** X is a Fake Out / Volt Tackle anti-terrain lead that shuts down the Indeedee + Sneasler and Rillaboom modes.
- Pair it with a Prankster Tailwind user such as Whimsicott. That flips the 4 faster threats and covers Trick Room.
- Add a Levitate or Flying partner, or Intimidate Incineroar, to handle Garchomp's Earthquake.
- A Helping Hand partner converts the point-5 numbers into KOs. X also carries Helping Hand itself if it needs to support.

**Pre-empting the case for Y: "200 Speed and a Zap Cannon that always paralyses."** Both are true, but they matter less than they look:
1. Paralysis from Zap Cannon hits one target per turn. X already has Electroweb and Thunder Wave, and Tailwind (Whimsicott, Pelipper and M-Staraptor are all on the board) makes Y's raw Speed tier moot.
2. No Guard works both ways. Every Rock Slide, Blizzard and Hurricane aimed at Y also cannot miss.
3. Y needs Life Orb to reach 11 OHKOs, only 2 more than Life Orb X's 9. Meanwhile, 11 of 21 threats OHKO Y against 5 for X, a 6-threat gap in X's favour.
4. Y cannot touch Psychic or Grassy Terrain. Its paralysis turn runs into an Indeedee-boosted spread Expanding Force (54.7-65.7% on Y) or a Grassy Wood Hammer (165.7-196.4%).

Speed only helps a Pokemon that survives the turn. On the M-C board, X is the form that survives.

### For Mega Raichu Y
**Thesis:** Mega Raichu Y is the better Mega Raichu for Reg M-C doubles. It is faster than all 21 threats on the board. With No Guard its moves never miss, so it can hit first and cripple what it doesn't KO. Mega Raichu X loses the Speed race to 4 of those threats and has to accept recoil and Electric Terrain to get its numbers.

**1. It moves first against the whole board (fact sheet).** Timid Mega Y reaches 200 Speed. The sheet lists "Board threats faster than it at max: none." Jolly Mega X tops out at 178 and loses to Sneasler 189, Whimsicott 184, M-Froslass 189 and M-Staraptor 178 (that last one is a Speed tie). Sneasler is the #2 threat at 34.29% usage. Against it, X has to take Close Combat for 78.8–92.7% before it can act. Y simply attacks first.

**2. Zap Cannon never misses, so paralysis is guaranteed (fact sheet; confirmed-facts.md).** No Guard turns a 120 BP move that always paralyses into 100% accuracy. Every target it doesn't KO is paralysed at half Speed. That speed control stays after Raichu faints, and Taunt and Tailwind don't undo it. Mega X has no ability that does this. Its Electric Terrain boost lasts five turns and then it's gone.

**3. It covers the Steels and Dark types that wall X (fact sheet).** Focus Blast is normally 70% accurate but always hits here:
- Archaludon: Y OHKOs it (124.9–147%). X does 39.8–47.5%.
- M-Tyranitar: Y OHKOs it (131.4–156.5%). X does 59.9–71.5%.
- Kingambit: Y does 87.9–104.3% (4/16 rolls KO). X does 56.5–66.7%.
- M-Baxcalibur: Y does 76.7–91.3%. X does 50.5–60.2% with a 75%-accurate Iron Tail.

Surf also OHKOs Arcanine-Hisui (125.6–148.8%). Every Y number is at 100% accuracy with no recoil. X's damage comes mostly from Volt Tackle, which costs it a third of the damage dealt as recoil.

**4. Helping Hand turns it into a KO machine against the top of the usage list (my engine run, Timid 2/0/0/32/0/32).** These are all guaranteed KOs:
- Zap Cannon: Sneasler (34.29%) 137.6–161.1%, M-Salamence (23.15%) 104.8–124.2%, Gholdengo 114.2–135.5%, Milotic 124.8–148.5%, M-Froslass 122.4–144.9%.
- Focus Blast: Incineroar (26.77%) 104–123.3%, Kingambit (22.44%) 131.9–156.5%, M-Baxcalibur 115–136.9%.
- Alluring Voice: Garchomp 100.5–118.4%.

Indeedee-F is close to a KO at 13/16 rolls. That covers 3 of the 5 most-used threats. X, even in its own terrain, doesn't KO Incineroar (70.3–83.7%), Kingambit or M-Salamence (58.6–69.9%).

**5. It can wait to Mega Evolve at no cost (confirmed-facts.md, Worlds 2026 reports).** Base Raichu and Mega Y have exactly the same HP, Defence and Special Defence (60/55/80). Y can start as base Raichu, keep Lightning Rod to pull in the opponent's Electric attacks, and Mega Evolve on the turn it wants. It also keeps Fake Out and Helping Hand. That flexibility is why Mega Raichu Y showed up in Worlds 2026 team reports.

**Best role and partners.** Play it as a fast lead that paralyses or picks off threats. Pair it with a Helping Hand partner, such as Whimsicott or anything else that offers Helping Hand plus a Ground immunity or redirection, so the Helping Hand KOs in point 4 are on the table from turn 1. Raichu covers Ground-weak Steels and slow Dark types with Focus Blast, and Zap Cannon handles speed control. That frees the partner from needing Tailwind. Y outspeeds the whole board already, so the team can spend its other slots on bulk.

**Pre-empting X's best argument: bulk and raw OHKOs.** X's strongest numbers are real. In the fact sheet's calcs, 5 of 21 threats OHKO X and 11 of 21 OHKO Y. X gets 6 OHKOs with no item, against Y's 5. Two things make this matter less:
- **X's OHKOs mostly land on frail targets.** Three of its six (Pelipper, Milotic, M-Staraptor) are ones Y also KOs or nearly KOs. Its Whimsicott OHKO relies on a 75%-accurate Iron Tail, and it hits Sneasler and M-Froslass only after they've already moved. Its biggest numbers need its own Electric Terrain and Volt Tackle recoil.
- **Bulk matters less when you act first.** A Pokemon that moves before everything, and paralyses or KOs its target, takes fewer hits. Five of Y's 11 OHKO-ers (Garchomp, Kingambit, Incineroar, M-Salamence, Gholdengo) are threats Y KOs first with Helping Hand. Most of the rest are paralysed after one Zap Cannon. X takes less damage per hit, but it takes more hits because it moves second into Sneasler and Whimsicott.

In this metagame, moving first and never missing is worth more than extra bulk. Mega Raichu Y is the better Mega form.

## Round 2 — Rebuttals
### Mega Raichu X responds
**Rebuttal: Mega Raichu X**

**What I concede.** Y's Speed claim is correct. At 200 Speed it outspeeds all 21 threats, and X at 178 loses to Sneasler, Whimsicott and M-Froslass and ties M-Staraptor. Y's No Guard Focus Blast also covers what X can't: it OHKOs Archaludon (124.9–147%) and M-Tyranitar (131.4–156.5%), where X does 39.8–47.5% and 59.9–71.5%. Y's Helping Hand Focus Blast on Kingambit (131.9–156.5%) checks out in my engine run. Y can also hold its Mega for free, and X can't do that without giving up its bulk.

**1. "It moves first against the whole board" skips priority and Trick Room.** Speed does nothing against priority, and the board's top threats carry it. My engine runs:
- Rillaboom's Grassy Glide on Y: 76.6–91.2%.
- Kingambit's Sucker Punch on Y: 79.6–94.2%.
- Incineroar's Fake Out on Y: 21.9–26.3%.

A Fake Out plus a Grassy Glide adds up to 98.5–117.5% on Y. That is a likely KO before Y's 200 Speed matters. The same pair does about 53–64% to X, and X's Electric Surge removes Grassy Glide's priority in the first place. Indeedee-F and Farigiraf also both set Trick Room. Under Trick Room, Y's Speed becomes a drawback, and Y has nothing to push back against Psychic Terrain.

**2. "Guaranteed paralysis" is guaranteed only for accuracy.**
- Garchomp is **immune** to Zap Cannon (engine: IMMUNE). Garchomp is also the threat whose Earthquake does 193.4–229.9% to Y.
- Zap Cannon hits one target per turn and takes Y's attacking slot for that turn.
- No Guard also works for the opponent. Anything aimed at Y cannot miss.

"Most of the rest are paralysed after one Zap Cannon" is really several turns, and Y has to survive each of them. Eleven of 21 threats OHKO Y.

**3. The Helping Hand KO list needs two actions every turn.** Each number in Y's point 4 depends on:
- the partner spending its turn on Helping Hand;
- Y not being hit by Fake Out.

Any Fake Out cancels the whole combo, and Incineroar and Sneasler are two of the five most-used threats on the board. X gets the same boost from Helping Hand. In its own terrain, Life Orb Volt Tackle with Helping Hand KOs Kingambit (110.1–130%), M-Salamence (114.5–136%), M-Metagross, Farigiraf and Indeedee-F. On M-Salamence and Kingambit, the gap Y cites comes from comparing X with no support against Y with Helping Hand. That comparison isn't fair.

**4. Why bulk still decides it.**
- Y's own sheet lists Sneasler Close Combat at 120.4–142.3% and M-Golisopod First Impression at 143.1–168.6% on Y.
- X survives Sneasler's hit (78.8–92.7%). Unboosted, it KOs Sneasler back with Volt Tackle (131.8–154.8%).
- Y's no-item Zap Cannon on Sneasler is only 8/16 to KO.

So Y's Speed edge against Sneasler is a coin flip, and when it fails Y is knocked out. X gives up the first hit, survives it, and wins the exchange every time.

Mega Raichu X still wins. Priority, Trick Room and Fake Out take away the Speed that Y's whole case rests on, while X survives that same pressure, removes Grassy Glide and Psychic Terrain on entry, and gets the same Helping Hand KOs.

### Mega Raichu Y responds
**Rebuttal for Mega Raichu Y**

**Concessions first.** X's terrain point is real. Electric Surge fires when X Mega Evolves, which is after the lead abilities, so it overwrites Grassy and Psychic Terrain. That takes Grassy Glide's priority away and brings grounded Fake Out back. Y cannot do this. X is also the bulkier form: 5 board threats OHKO X and 11 OHKO Y. I also concede that No Guard works both ways, so M-Froslass's Blizzard and any Rock Slide aimed at Y will not miss. Finally, I reran X's Life Orb + Helping Hand Volt Tackle numbers and they are correct: Kingambit 110.1–130%, M-Salamence 114.5–136%, Indeedee-F 142.9–168.9%.

**1. Points 3 and 6 need two different Pokemon.** The bulk in point 3 comes from Adamant 32 HP / 32 Atk / 2 Def, which leaves 0 Speed. I ran that spread through the engine and it has **130 Speed**, not 178. At 130 Speed, nine board threats move before it: Sneasler, Whimsicott, M-Froslass, M-Staraptor, Garchomp, M-Salamence, Arcanine-H, M-Metagross and Gholdengo. The bulk doesn't even solve Garchomp: its Earthquake still does 103–121.6% (a KO), and X's own point 3 lists that line twice. X has to choose. It can be the 178-Speed attacker that takes 78.8–92.7% from Sneasler, or the bulky one that gets outsped by nine threats. It cannot be both. Y is at 200 Speed on every set.

**2. X's Helping Hand KOs cost a lot of its own HP.** Volt Tackle recoil is one third of the damage dealt, and Life Orb takes another 10% of X's HP. My numbers here use board HP (Kingambit 207, M-Salamence 186) and X's 137 HP:
- KOing Kingambit costs X about 82 HP, roughly 60% of its health.
- KOing M-Salamence costs about 55%.

So X's "survives the turn" bulk is spent by its own attacks. Y's Helping Hand KOs (Zap Cannon on Sneasler and M-Salamence, Focus Blast on Incineroar and Kingambit) have no recoil, no item and never miss. X's plan also uses up the team's one Life Orb under Item Clause.

**3. The terrain point is narrower than it looks.**
- **Rillaboom:** Its Wood Hammer only fails to KO X because X Mega Evolved on turn 1. X then takes 83.9–99.3% plus recoil, so the matchup is survival, not a win.
- **Indeedee-F:** Y outspeeds it and Zap Cannon paralyses it (64.4–76.3%) before Expanding Force goes off. Expanding Force does only 54.7–65.7% to Y anyway, so Y survives it.
- **Tailwind:** X's answer to its Speed problem is a Prankster Tailwind from Whimsicott. That uses a partner's turn on something Y's Speed already gives it.
- **Y's Life Orb:** "Y needs Life Orb to reach 11 OHKOs" misstates my case. My KO list uses Helping Hand and no item, which leaves the Life Orb free for a teammate.

Mega Raichu Y still wins because it moves first on every set, never misses and takes no recoil, while X's bulk, Speed and damage each come from a build or item that rules out one of the others.

## ⚖️ The Judge's Opinion
_Having heard both openings and both rebuttals, Rotom Judge rules on which form is better, and why._

Almost every damage roll either side cited reproduces. The case turns on which premises survived checking.

**A's damage case falls apart on items.** Points 4 and 5 (9 OHKOs, and the Helping Hand KOs on Kingambit, M-Salamence and M-Metagross) all need a Life Orb that Mega Raichu X can't hold. A's rebuttal claimed Helping Hand gives X "the same KOs" as Y. On the legal stone that is false:

| Target (HH, Electric Terrain) | X Volt Tackle | Y |
|---|---|---|
| Kingambit | 1/16 | KO (Focus Blast) |
| M-Salamence | 5/16 | KO (Zap Cannon) |
| M-Metagross | 2/16 | — |

A's line on Sneasler, "wins the exchange every time", ignores Volt Tackle recoil. X dies in that trade.

**A's real, proven points:**
- **Terrain denial.** Mega Evolving on turn 1 overwrites Grassy or Psychic Terrain.
  - Wood Hammer falls from a KO to 83.9-99.3%.
  - Grassy Glide loses priority.
  - Expanding Force drops to 33.6-40.1% and is no longer a spread move.
  - Fake Out works again.
- **Double the bulk.** X is OHKO'd by 5/21 board threats; Y by 11/21.
- **Priority punishes Y.** Grassy Glide + Fake Out does 98.5-117.5% to Y and about 53-64% to X. First Impression and Garchomp's EQ OHKO Y.

**B's case held.**
- Y moves first against the whole board on its standard spread.
- No Guard makes Zap Cannon guaranteed paralysis.
- Focus Blast covers the Steel and Dark walls that X can't touch (Archaludon, M-Tyranitar, Kingambit).
- Its Helping Hand KO list is all legal and correct. It reaches 3 of the top 5 threats (Sneasler, Incineroar, Kingambit) plus M-Salamence and Garchomp.
- B's best rebuttal was the Speed catch. A's bulk spread and A's 178-Speed spread are different Pokemon.

**B's weak spots:**
- It accepted A's illegal Life Orb numbers.
- It oversold "moves first on every set". Priority from Rillaboom, Kingambit, Incineroar, M-Golisopod and Arcanine-H ignores Speed, and so does an Unburden Sneasler under Psychic Terrain (378 Spe).
- It never answered Rillaboom. Wood Hammer does 165.7-196.4% to Y, and 127.7-151.1% even outside Grassy Terrain.

**What each form is for:**
- **Mega Y** is the better Mega. It hits harder across the board, has wider legal Helping Hand coverage, gives guaranteed Speed control, and costs nothing to hold as base Raichu. The Worlds 2026 reports confirm this, and they played it as support/speed control.
- **Mega X** is a narrower anti-terrain Fake Out lead. It is the better pick only if a team specifically needs to deny Rillaboom and Indeedee terrain.

### 🏆 Conclusion
**Rotom Judge rules that Mega Raichu Y is the better form** (Mega Raichu X 63, Mega Raichu Y 79).

Mega Raichu Y wins, 79 to 63.

A's terrain and bulk numbers all reproduce. Mega Evolving on turn 1 cuts Grassy Wood Hammer from a KO to 83.9-99.3%, and only 5/21 board threats OHKO X against 11/21 for Y. But A's damage case (points 4 and 5, and the rebuttal's claim that Helping Hand gives X "the same KOs") relies on a Life Orb that Mega X can't hold, because it has to hold Raichunite X. On the legal stone, Helping Hand Volt Tackle is only 1/16 on Kingambit, 5/16 on M-Salamence and 2/16 on M-Metagross, and at -1 from Intimidate every claimed KO disappears. A also claimed X "wins the exchange every time" against Sneasler, but X dies to Volt Tackle recoil: 52 HP of recoil against the 10-29 HP it has left after Close Combat.

Every one of B's Helping Hand numbers checks out on a legal item, and they are guaranteed KOs:
- Zap Cannon: Sneasler 137.6-161.1%, M-Salamence 104.8-124.2%.
- Focus Blast: Incineroar 104-123.3%, Kingambit 131.9-156.5%.
- Alluring Voice: Garchomp 100.5-118.4%.
It also found that A's bulk spread only has 130 Speed. B's losses were accepting A's illegal Life Orb numbers and overselling "moves first". Priority ignores Y's Speed (Grassy Glide + Fake Out does 98.5-117.5% to Y), and Unburden Sneasler reaches 378 Speed under Psychic Terrain.

Y is the better Mega: a fast support and breaker that paralyses on every hit and punches up with Helping Hand. X is a narrower anti-terrain Fake Out lead against the Rillaboom and Indeedee modes.

## The teams
### Built around Mega Raichu X
**Judge's brief.** Anti-terrain Fake Out lead holding Raichunite X (it can NOT hold a Life Orb). Jolly 2 HP/32 Atk/32 Spe (178 Spe). Moves: Fake Out / Volt Tackle / Protect plus Helping Hand, Electroweb or Brick Break. Mega Evolve on turn 1: Electric Surge overwrites Rillaboom's Grassy Terrain and Indeedee-F's Psychic Terrain before anyone moves, and it re-fires on every switch-in. That removes Grassy Glide's priority, makes Expanding Force single-target and weaker, and lets Fake Out hit grounded targets again. Do not run the 32 HP/32 Atk/2 Def bulk spread: it drops X to 130 Spe (9 threats outspeed it) and still loses to Garchomp's Earthquake (103-121.6%).
