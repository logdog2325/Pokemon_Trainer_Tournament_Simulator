# Feraligatr Teambuilding — Step 5: Five teams to test

Every team below is built from the partners (step 3) and cores (step 4) in the doc, and every one passed the Champions legality check (`rotom-judge/validate.js`): species, items, moves, abilities, 66 stat points, Item Clause, one Mega per battle.

| # | Team | Cores used (step 4) | Second Mega | How Feraligatr wins |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Grassy Fake Out (control) | 4 Rillaboom, 14 Sneasler, 9 Kingambit | Lucario Z | Double Fake Out buys a Dragon Dance turn |
| 2 | Rain Tailwind | 1 Pelipper, 5 Pelipper+Arch, 11 Pelipper+Golisopod | Golisopod | Rain + Tailwind, no setup needed |
| 3 | Psychic Terrain redirection | 2 Indeedee, 10 Indeedee+Gardevoir | Gardevoir | Follow Me + priority immunity while it Dragon Dances |
| 4 | Screens + Tailwind | 7 Grimmsnarl, 16 Talonflame, 17 Weavile+Blaziken | Blaziken | Swords Dance behind Reflect / Light Screen |
| 5 | Aurora Veil snow | 6 Ninetales-A, 13 Weavile | Froslass | Dragon Dance behind Aurora Veil |
