Source: **Serebii** (https://www.serebii.net/pokemonchampions/items.shtml), fetched 2026-06,

Matters for **Item Clause** (every item on a team must be unique).

## Reg M-C additions (12) — live 2026-09-09
Leek · **Rocky Helmet** · **Air Balloon** · Red Card · Binding Band · Eject Button · Normal Gem ·
**Terrain Extender** · **Electric Seed** · **Psychic Seed** · **Misty Seed** · **Grassy Seed**
- **Terrain Extender**: your terrain lasts 8 turns instead of 5. Does not protect it — a later
  setter still overwrites you.
- **Seeds**: consumed on entering the matching terrain. Grassy/Electric → +1 Def, Psychic/Misty → +1 SpD.
  The consumption is what makes them **Unburden** fuel (Sneasler, Thievul, Hawlucha).
- **Air Balloon**: Ground immunity until hit — and it also makes the holder **ungrounded**, so it
  gives up every terrain boost and terrain protection while it is held.
- **Rocky Helmet**: 1/6 recoil on contact. Was absent before M-C.
